Macro backdrop

How the model is learning

Each forecast is checked against the actual rate when its time comes. A miss earns penalty points: the squared error measured in typical moves, one extra point for calling the direction wrong, and half a point when the outcome lands outside the 80% range. Penalised examples then weigh more in the next update and are replayed more often, experts that keep missing lose influence, and the learning rate rises while penalties run above normal.

    Assumptions and calibration

    The landed 24K rate is spot gold × USD/INR ÷ 31.1035 g × 10 × (1 + import duty) × (1 + local premium). Change anything here and the board updates at once. Values are saved in this browser.

    Enter the rate your jeweller or IBJA quotes, without GST, and press Calibrate. The difference becomes the local premium.

    How it works

    1. Data. Every minute the page pulls 1-minute candles for gold, silver, Brent crude, the S&P 500, the euro and the yen (Hyperliquid's trade.xyz markets), a US 10-year Treasury-yield market and bitcoin. It adds spot gold from gold-api.com, USD/INR from three sources, US Treasury nominal and real yields, earthquakes from USGS and world news volume and tone from GDELT.
    2. Indices. Each input becomes a 0–100 index. News indices compare the latest hour with the past week. Geological risk adds up recent earthquakes, weighted by magnitude and distance to the main gold-mining belts. Festive demand follows the Hindu calendar and the wedding seasons.
    3. Deep net. A two-layer LSTM reads the last hour as 12 steps of 36 features, a dense branch reads the current minute, and the network outputs a mean and an uncertainty for 1, 5, 15 and 60 minutes ahead. It first trains on 3½ days of history, then keeps training every minute with TensorFlow.js on your device.
    4. Ensemble. The deep net competes with a factor model (recursive least squares), momentum, mean reversion and a no-change forecast. Weights follow the Hedge rule: every penalty point shrinks an expert's weight, so the blend shifts toward whoever has been right lately.
    5. Self-correction. Penalties raise sample weights and replay priority, move the learning rate, widen or narrow the 80% range until it really covers 80%, and trigger a learning boost when a drift detector sees penalties staying high.
    6. Memory. The model, ledger and settings live in this browser's storage, so learning resumes where it stopped. Nothing is sent anywhere.

    Limits

    • Minute-to-minute gold moves are mostly noise. A live skill near 0% against the no-change forecast is normal; read the forecast as a probability, not a promise.
    • The model only learns while this page is open. Browsers slow down background tabs, and the page catches up when you return.
    • Free feeds can lag, rate-limit or disappear. News indices refresh every 15 minutes at best, and India's 10-year yield has no free live feed, so it is entered by hand.
    • Shop rates add dealer premiums or discounts, making charges and GST. Calibrate to the rate you are quoted.
    • This is a research tool, not investment advice.

    Sources: Hyperliquid (trade.xyz gold, silver, Brent, S&P 500, EUR, JPY; US 10-year yield market), gold-api.com, Coinbase, ExchangeRate-API, Frankfurter (ECB), US Treasury, USGS, GDELT. Deep learning by TensorFlow.js.
